I’d prioritize these improvements:
1. Recurring transactions — automatically track rent, subscriptions, salary, and regular bills.
2. Budgets by category — set monthly limits and show how much remains, with alerts when spending approaches the limit.
3. Upcoming payments — show bills and loan payments due soon, with optional reminders.
4. Better loan tracking — support partial payments, remaining balances, due dates, and payment history.
5. Clearer financial insights — compare months, highlight spending changes, and distinguish income, spending, savings, and debt payments.
6. Safer CSV imports — preview transactions, map columns, and detect duplicates before saving.
7. Savings goals — track progress toward an emergency fund, purchase, or other target.
8. Account security — verify email addresses during signup and offer password changes within account settings. Your current signup treats addresses as confirmed without verification.
My first picks would be recurring transactions, budgets, and upcoming payments because they make the app more useful day to day.
